Inference Reading Formula

Text Clue + What I Already Know = Smart Inference

  • Literal Fact: Directly stated on the page (not an inference).
  • Smart Inference: Logical conclusion supported by exact clues + real-world knowledge.
  • Trap Answers: Watch out for choices that merely copy a literal detail, contradict the text, or guess beyond the clues.

Speaking Frame: "We infer that _____ because the text says '_____,' and we know that _____."
